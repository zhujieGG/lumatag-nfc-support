Device and hardware requirements

NFC read and write operations require an NFC-capable iPhone and compatible pool-light hardware. Hardware and firmware must support the app's configuration protocol; a generic NFC tag is not sufficient.

LumaTag NFC is designed for NFC-capable iPhones. iPad is not supported.

Compatibility varies by light model and firmware. Contact support if you are unsure whether your light is supported.

Read current settings

  1. Open LumaTag NFC on an NFC-capable iPhone.
  2. Tap Read Light Settings.
  3. Follow the NFC prompt and hold the iPhone's NFC antenna near the light's designated scan location.
  4. After a successful read, the controller compatibility mode and white color temperature selections update to match the light.

Apply a setting

Controller compatibility: Choose a supported mode, tap Apply Control Mode, and scan the light.

White color temperature: Select 2200K, 2700K, 3000K, 4000K, or 5000K, tap Apply Color Temperature, and scan the light.

The settings can be applied independently. Selecting an option alone does not write to the light. Read the settings again to verify a change.

Troubleshooting

  • If the app reports that NFC is unavailable, use an NFC-capable iPhone.
  • Follow the hardware manufacturer's preparation and scan-location instructions.
  • If a scan fails, retry and keep the iPhone close to the designated NFC location until the operation completes.
  • Confirm the light model and firmware are compatible. The app does not provide universal remote control of pool automation systems.
