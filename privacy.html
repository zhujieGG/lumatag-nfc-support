Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 26, 2026

This policy describes the handling of information by LumaTag NFC and this support website. Contact jay892023740@gmail.com with privacy questions.

Lighting configuration and NFC

The app reads and writes configuration information on compatible pool-light hardware over NFC. Configuration information includes controller compatibility mode and white color temperature. The app processes this information locally to display the settings and perform the changes you request.

The app does not transmit this lighting configuration information to a developer-operated server. NFC access is used when you initiate a read or apply operation on a compatible iPhone.

Accounts, advertising, and analytics

The app does not require an account for its lighting setup features. The current app does not include advertising or developer-operated analytics or tracking services. It does not upload your contact information, location, or device identifiers to the developer.

Support email

If you contact support by email, we receive your email address and any information you choose to include. We use this information to respond to your request and investigate the reported issue. Please do not send passwords or sensitive information that is not needed for support.

To ask about or request deletion of your support correspondence, email jay892023740@gmail.com.

The hosting provider's own policies apply to that processing.
